# Epidemic Watch Dashboard

Building AI course final project

## Summary

Epidemic Watch Dashboard is an AI-assisted platform that gathers epidemiological information from multiple trusted public health sources and presents it in a single, easy-to-understand view. The goal is to help healthcare professionals monitor infectious disease situations more efficiently by reducing the need to manually search and compare information from multiple websites and reports.

## Background

Monitoring infectious diseases often requires consulting multiple organizations and information sources, such as the World Health Organization (WHO), the European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC), the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), and national public health agencies.

This process can be time-consuming because relevant information is scattered across different websites, reports, dashboards, and publications. Healthcare professionals must often collect, compare, and interpret information from several sources before obtaining a complete picture of the current epidemiological situation.

This problem occurs regularly in clinical and public health work. My personal motivation comes from seeing how much time is spent searching for information that already exists but is difficult to access in a centralized and user-friendly manner. A unified dashboard could significantly improve situational awareness and support faster access to relevant information.

## Data Sources and AI Methods

The project would use publicly available epidemiological data and reports from trusted organizations, including:

- World Health Organization (WHO)
- European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC)
- U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)
- Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare (THL)
- Other national and international public health organizations

The AI component would not replace official sources. Instead, it would assist users by collecting, organizing, filtering, and summarizing information from those sources.

Potential AI techniques include:

- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Automated text summarization
- Information extraction from reports and bulletins
- Trend identification
- Anomaly detection for unusual disease activity
- AI-generated situation summaries

The system could allow users to select a disease, pathogen, geographic region, or country and receive a consolidated overview of the current situation.

## How Is It Used?

The primary users would be healthcare professionals, infectious disease specialists, epidemiologists, public health authorities, and other experts who need up-to-date information about disease outbreaks and epidemiological trends.

Users would select a pathogen, disease, geographic region, or country of interest. The dashboard would then gather relevant information from multiple trusted sources and present it in a unified visual interface.

The goal is not to replace expert judgment but to support information gathering and situational awareness. By reducing the time required to search multiple sources manually, professionals can focus more on analysis and decision-making.

## Challenges

Several challenges must be considered.

Different organizations publish data in different formats and update their information at different intervals. As a result, combining information into a single view may be technically difficult.

Data quality may vary between countries and organizations. Reporting delays, incomplete datasets, and differences in surveillance systems can affect the accuracy of the information presented.

AI-generated summaries may occasionally omit important details or misinterpret the context of a report. Therefore, users should always have access to the original sources and verify important findings directly.

Not all epidemiological information is publicly available. Some important datasets may be restricted or accessible only to authorized organizations.

Finally, the dashboard should not be used as the sole basis for clinical or public health decisions. It is intended as a decision-support and information-discovery tool rather than a replacement for expert evaluation.

## What Next?

Future development could include:

- Automated alerts for emerging outbreaks
- Geographic risk maps
- Historical trend analysis
- Predictive models for disease spread
- Comparison of epidemiological situations between countries
- Personalized monitoring dashboards
- Daily AI-generated epidemiological briefings

As more users adopt the platform and provide feedback, the system could evolve into a comprehensive situational awareness tool for infectious disease monitoring.
